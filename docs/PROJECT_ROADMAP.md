# Cybersecurity Planet - Project Roadmap



## 1. Project Overview



Cybersecurity Planet is a public educational platform designed to help people become safer, more informed, and more confident digital citizens.



The platform combines beginner-friendly educational content with practical interactive tools covering cybersecurity, privacy, artificial intelligence, media literacy, digital citizenship, and digital health.



The goal is not simply to provide information. Cybersecurity Planet should help users understand digital risks, assess their own habits, and take practical steps to improve their digital safety.



---



## 2. Vision



To make cybersecurity and digital literacy education understandable, practical, and accessible to everyday internet users.



---



## 3. Mission



Cybersecurity Planet empowers people to make safer and better-informed decisions online through:



- Beginner-friendly education

- Interactive assessments and tools

- Practical cybersecurity guidance

- Privacy awareness

- AI literacy

- Media and misinformation literacy

- Responsible digital citizenship

- Digital health awareness



---



## 4. Core Principles



Cybersecurity Planet should be:



### Educational

Every feature should help the user learn something useful.



### Practical

Users should leave with knowledge or actions they can apply in real life.



### Beginner-Friendly

Content should not assume advanced technical knowledge.



### Privacy-Conscious

Tools should avoid unnecessarily collecting or storing sensitive user information.



### Accessible

Content and interfaces should be usable by people with different levels of technical knowledge and ability.



### Trustworthy

Educational material should be accurate, responsible, and clearly presented.



### Human-Centred

Technology should serve people rather than overwhelm them.
